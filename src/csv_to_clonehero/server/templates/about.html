ABOUT RIFF

Our mission

Riff is a free community tool that compares your music list with community-built charts on Enchor. You stay in control of which chart versions join your Clone Hero or YARG library.

Support the people behind the game

Clone Hero is a free, fan-made rhythm game built by one dedicated creator and the community. That creator is currently fighting cancer, and the game lives on because of people who give back. If you love what we all play, please consider chipping in.

How we respect you and this community

  • Public chart ZIPs are transient and stream to your computer; local folder builds store files only in the destination you choose.
  • Your Spotify refresh token is encrypted at rest and used only to find songs.
  • This site runs ads to cover hosting; the ad experience is meant to stay tasteful.
