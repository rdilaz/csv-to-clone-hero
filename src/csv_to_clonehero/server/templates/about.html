Our mission

CSV to Clone Hero is a free community tool that finds every song in your Spotify library that already has a playable chart on Chorus Encore, so you can drop a complete, organized collection into Clone Hero or YARG.

Support the people behind the game

Clone Hero is a free, fan-made rhythm game built by one dedicated creator and the community. That creator is currently fighting cancer, and the game lives on because of people who give back. If you love what we all play, please consider chipping in.

How we respect you and this community

  • We never store chart files. Downloads stream to your computer.
