Add an Apple Music export

In Music or iTunes on your computer, select a playlist and choose File > Library > Export Playlist. Choose TXT or XML, up to 10 MB.

Apple's export instructions

Imports song names, artists and albums only. One export becomes one playlist; XML playlist groups are not restored. A new export replaces your previous Apple Music import. No account access or Apple Music audio is imported. Find charts is a separate step.
